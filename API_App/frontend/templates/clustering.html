Buoy Similarity Analysis

KMeans clustering groups buoys with similar meteorological behavior. Each buoy is described by the mean and standard deviation of WVHT, WTMP, WSPD, PRES and ATMP — 10 features in total. StandardScaler normalizes all features before clustering. The best number of clusters k is chosen automatically via the Silhouette Score.

Static Clustering — Overall Buoy Profiles

Each point in the scatter represents one buoy, described by its average conditions over the entire observation period. Buoys close together in the PCA space have similar long-term meteorological profiles.

PCA Scatter Plot

PCA reduces the 10 features to 2 dimensions for visualization. Each labeled point is a buoy. Distance between points reflects meteorological similarity — buoys far apart have different wave/temperature regimes.

Silhouette Score by k

The silhouette score measures how well each buoy fits its assigned cluster vs the nearest other cluster. Values range from -1 to 1 — higher is better. The blue bar is the automatically selected k with the highest score.

Elbow Plot — Inertia by k

Inertia measures the total distance of buoys from their cluster centroid. It always decreases as k increases, but the rate slows — the "elbow" point suggests a good k. Used alongside the silhouette score to confirm the choice.
